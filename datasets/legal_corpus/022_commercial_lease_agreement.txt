COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement (“Lease”) is made and entered into as of the 15th day of January, 2025 (“Effective Date”), by and between Redwood Properties LLC, a New York limited liability company having an address at 100 Maple Avenue, Albany, NY 12207 (“Landlord”), and Silverline Technologies Inc., a New York corporation having an address at 250 Franklin Street, Suite 400, New York, NY 10013 (“Tenant”).

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Landlord is the owner of certain commercial real property located at 450 East 65th Street, New York, New York 10065 (the “Premises”);

WHEREAS, Tenant desires to lease the Premises from Landlord for commercial office use, and Landlord agrees to lease the Premises to Tenant under the terms and conditions set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 “Premises” means the approximate 5,000 rentable square feet located on the 3rd floor of the building at 450 East 65th Street, New York, NY 10065, as depicted in Exhibit A attached hereto.

1.2 “Lease Term” means the period beginning on February 1, 2025 (“Commencement Date”) and ending on January 31, 2028 (“Termination Date”), unless sooner terminated pursuant to this Lease.

1.3 “Base Rent” means the fixed monthly rent amount payable by Tenant as set forth in Section 3.1.

1.4 “Late Payment Penalty” means an additional charge of two percent (2%) per month on any unpaid rent or other sums due under this Lease that are not received by Landlord within five (5) calendar days after the due date.

2. PREMISES AND USE

2.1 Lease of Premises. Landlord hereby leases to Tenant, and Tenant hereby leases from Landlord, the Premises for the Lease Term.

2.2 Permitted Use. Tenant shall use the Premises solely for general office purposes related to software development and associated administrative activities and for no other purpose without Landlord’s prior written consent.

3. RENT

3.1 Base Rent. Tenant shall pay to Landlord base rent in the amount of Forty Thousand Dollars ($40,000.00) per month during the Lease Term, payable monthly in advance on the first day of each calendar month at Landlord’s address or at such other place as Landlord may designate in writing.

3.2 Adjustment. Beginning on February 1, 2026, Base Rent shall increase by three percent (3%) annually, such adjustment to apply on each anniversary of the Commencement Date.

3.3 Late Payment Penalty. If Tenant fails to pay any installment of Base Rent or any other amount due hereunder within five (5) calendar days after the due date, Tenant shall pay to Landlord, as additional rent, a Late Payment Penalty of two percent (2%) per month (calculated daily from due date until payment in full) on the overdue amount. The Late Payment Penalty shall be deemed additional rent for purposes of this Lease.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT

4.1 Amount. Upon execution of this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of Forty Thousand Dollars ($40,000.00) as a security deposit (“Security Deposit”) to secure Tenant’s performance of its obligations hereunder.

4.2 Use and Return. Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to cure Tenant defaults or to compensate Landlord for damages resulting from Tenant’s breach of this Lease. Any unused portion of the Security Deposit shall be returned to Tenant within thirty (30) days after the Termination Date, provided Tenant has fully performed its obligations.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

5.1 Landlord’s Obligations. Landlord shall maintain the structural components of the Premises, including the roof, exterior walls, and foundation, and ensure that common areas are clean, safe, and in good repair.

5.2 Tenant’s Obligations. Tenant shall maintain the interior of the Premises in good order and repair, including any fixtures, equipment, and improvements installed by Tenant. Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord of any condition requiring repair.

6. UTILITIES AND SERVICES

Tenant shall be responsible for all utility charges related to the Premises, including electricity, water, gas, telephone, internet, and any other services used by Tenant. Landlord shall not be liable for any interruption or failure of utilities.

7. INSURANCE

7.1 Tenant Insurance. Tenant shall maintain, at its expense, commercial general liability insurance with a minimum combined single limit of $1,000,000 per occurrence and property insurance covering Tenant’s personal property and leasehold improvements.

7.2 Proof of Insurance. Tenant shall provide Landlord with certificates of insurance evidencing compliance with this Section upon execution of this Lease and upon renewal of such policies.

8. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet the Premises or any portion thereof without Landlord’s prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

9. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

9.1 Events of Default. Tenant shall be in default under this Lease if Tenant fails to pay rent or any other amount due within ten (10) days after written notice of such failure or if Tenant breaches any other covenant and fails to cure within thirty (30) days after written notice thereof.

9.2 Remedies. Upon any default, Landlord may pursue all remedies available at law or in equity, including termination of this Lease and/or recovery of damages.

10. TERMINATION

10.1 Expiration. This Lease shall automatically terminate on the Termination Date unless extended by written agreement.

10.2 Early Termination. Tenant may terminate this Lease early only upon payment of an early termination fee equal to three (3) months’ Base Rent, provided Tenant gives Landlord at least ninety (90) days’ prior written notice.

11. SURRENDER OF PREMISES

At the expiration or termination of this Lease, Tenant shall surrender the Premises in broom-clean condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

12. NOTICES

All notices required or permitted under this Lease shall be in writing and delivered personally, by certified mail (return receipt requested), or by nationally recognized courier service to the parties at their respective addresses set forth above or such other address as a party may specify in writing.

13. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York without regard to conflict of laws principles. Any dispute arising hereunder shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in New York County, New York.

14. MISCELLANEOUS

14.1 Entire Agreement. This Lease constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the Premises and supersedes all prior negotiations or agreements.

14.2 Amendments. Any amendment to this Lease must be in writing and signed by both parties.

14.3 Severability. If any provision of this Lease is held unenforceable, the remainder shall remain in full force and effect.

14.4 Waiver. No waiver of any breach shall be deemed a waiver of subsequent breaches.
