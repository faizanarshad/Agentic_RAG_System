COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement (“Lease”) is made and entered into as of the 1st day of March, 2025 (“Effective Date”), by and between Apex Property Holdings, LLC, a Texas limited liability company (“Landlord”), and Meridian Technologies, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Tenant”).

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Landlord is the owner of certain commercial real property located at 1402 Canyon Ridge Drive, Suite 210, Austin, Travis County, Texas 78734 (the “Premises”); and

WHEREAS, Tenant desires to lease the Premises from Landlord, and Landlord agrees to lease the Premises to Tenant, pursuant to the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 “Commencement Date” means March 15, 2025.

1.2 “Lease Term” means the period beginning on the Commencement Date and ending at 11:59 p.m. on March 14, 2028, unless terminated earlier pursuant to the terms of this Lease.

1.3 “Base Rent” means the fixed monthly rent payable by Tenant to Landlord as set forth in Section 3.1.

1.4 “Permitted Use” means general office, research and development, and administrative functions consistent with local zoning ordinances.

1.5 “Security Deposit” means the amount deposited by Tenant to secure performance under this Lease as described in Section 5.2.

2. PREMISES AND LEASE TERM

2.1 Leased Premises. Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant leases from Landlord, approximately 5,200 rentable square feet of office space located within the building at 1402 Canyon Ridge Drive, Suite 210, Austin, Texas.

2.2 Lease Term. The Lease Term shall commence on the Commencement Date and shall continue for a fixed term of three (3) years, ending on March 14, 2028.

2.3 Possession. Landlord shall deliver possession of the Premises to Tenant in broom-clean condition, free of tenants and liens, on the Commencement Date.

3. RENT

3.1 Base Rent. Tenant shall pay to Landlord monthly Base Rent of Five Thousand Four Hundred Dollars ($5,400.00), payable in advance on the first day of each calendar month during the Lease Term.

3.2 Additional Rent. Tenant shall pay Tenant’s Pro Rata Share of operating expenses, taxes, insurance, and maintenance costs related to the Premises as Additional Rent. Such amounts shall be reconciled annually, and Landlord shall provide Tenant with a statement of such charges within ninety (90) days after the end of each calendar year.

3.3 Late Payments. If Tenant fails to pay any amount of rent within five (5) business days after such amount is due, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to five percent (5%) of the overdue amount.

4. USE AND OCCUPANCY

4.1 Permitted Use. Tenant shall use and occupy the Premises solely for the Permitted Use and for no other purpose without prior written consent of Landlord, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

4.2 Compliance with Laws. Tenant shall comply with all applicable laws, ordinances, regulations, and requirements of governmental authorities pertaining to the use, maintenance, and occupancy of the Premises.

5. SECURITY DEPOSIT

5.1 Security Deposit Amount. Upon execution of this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of Ten Thousand Eight Hundred Dollars ($10,800.00) as a Security Deposit.

5.2 Use of Security Deposit. Landlord may use the Security Deposit to cure Tenant’s defaults under this Lease, including payment of unpaid rent or damages to the Premises. If the Security Deposit is applied, Tenant shall replenish it within ten (10) days of written demand.

5.3 Return of Security Deposit. Subject to Tenant’s performance and compliance with this Lease, Landlord shall return the Security Deposit, less any lawful deductions, within thirty (30) days after the expiration or earlier termination of this Lease.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

6.1 Landlord’s Obligations. Landlord shall maintain the structural components of the Premises, including roof, foundation, exterior walls, and common areas in good repair, reasonable wear and tear excepted.

6.2 Tenant’s Obligations. Tenant shall maintain the interior of the Premises in good repair and condition, including fixtures and equipment installed by Tenant. Tenant shall repair any damage caused by Tenant or Tenant’s agents or invitees.

6.3 Alterations. Tenant shall not make any alterations, additions, or improvements to the Premises without Landlord’s prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Any approved alterations shall comply with applicable laws and shall become the property of Landlord upon expiration of the Lease unless otherwise agreed in writing.

7. INSURANCE

7.1 Tenant Insurance. Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than One Million Dollars ($1,000,000) per occurrence and Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000) aggregate, naming Landlord as an additional insured. Tenant shall also maintain property insurance covering Tenant’s personal property and leasehold improvements.

7.2 Landlord Insurance. Landlord shall maintain insurance on the building and common areas against risks of fire, casualty, and other standard perils.

7.3 Certificates. Upon request, each party shall provide the other with certificates of insurance evidencing the required coverages.

8. INDEMNITY AND LIABILITY

8.1 Indemnity by Tenant. Tenant shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Landlord and Landlord’s agents from any claims, damages, liabilities, and expenses arising from Tenant’s use or occupancy of the Premises, except for Landlord’s gross negligence or willful misconduct.

8.2 Indemnity by Landlord. Landlord shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Tenant and Tenant’s agents from any claims arising from Landlord’s negligence or willful misconduct.

8.3 Mutual Liability Cap. Except for indemnity obligations arising from gross negligence or willful misconduct, each party’s total liability to the other for any claim arising under or related to this Lease shall be limited to the total amount of Base Rent paid by Tenant during the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

9. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

9.1 Tenant Defaults. Tenant shall be in default if Tenant fails to pay rent or other charges within ten (10) days after written notice of non-payment; or if Tenant breaches any other term of this Lease and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after written notice.

9.2 Landlord Remedies. Upon Tenant’s default, Landlord may terminate this Lease, pursue damages, reenter and take possession of the Premises, or exercise any other rights available under Texas law.

9.3 Landlord Defaults. Landlord shall be in default if Landlord fails to perform any material obligation under this Lease and does not cure such default within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant.

9.4 Tenant Remedies. Upon Landlord’s default, Tenant may pursue any legal and equitable remedies available under Texas law.

10. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

10.1 Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet the Premises without the prior written consent of Landlord, not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed. Any assignment or sublease without Landlord’s consent shall be void.

10.2 Tenant shall remain liable for all obligations under this Lease notwithstanding any assignment or sublease.

11. ENTRY AND INSPECTION

Landlord and its agents may enter the Premises during reasonable business hours upon reasonable prior notice to inspect, make repairs, or show the Premises to prospective tenants or purchasers, except in emergencies where no notice is required.

12. SURRENDER AND HOLDOVER

12.1 Surrender. Upon expiration or termination of this Lease, Tenant shall surrender the Premises in good condition, reasonable wear and tear excepted, and remove all personal property.

12.2 Holdover. Any holding over beyond the Lease Term without Landlord’s consent shall constitute a tenancy at sufferance and Tenant shall pay rent at one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the then-current Base Rent.

This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Any dispute arising out of or related to this Lease shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Travis County, Texas.

15. MISCELLANEOUS

15.1 Entire Agreement. This Lease contains the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations and agreements, oral or written.

15.2 Amendments. This Lease may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

15.3 Waiver. No waiver of any default or breach shall be deemed a waiver of any other or subsequent default or breach.

15.4 Severability. If any provision of this Lease is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

15.5 Successors and Assigns. This Lease shall bind and benefit the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.
