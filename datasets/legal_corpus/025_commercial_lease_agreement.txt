COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

THIS COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (“Lease”) is made as of the 15th day of April, 2025

BETWEEN:

Northern Pines Property Holdings Inc., an Ontario corporation having its principal place of business at 1200 Elm Street, Suite 400, Toronto, Ontario M5G 2L5 (hereinafter called the "Landlord")

- and -

Blue Horizon Outdoor Gear Ltd., an Ontario corporation having its principal place of business at 456 Lakeshore Drive, Unit 12, Mississauga, Ontario L5B 4R7 (hereinafter called the "Tenant")

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the premises located at 238 Commerce Way, Unit 8, Oakville, Ontario (the "Premises"); and

WHEREAS the Tenant desires to lease the Premises from the Landlord for the purpose of operating a retail outdoor equipment business;

NOW THEREFORE in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 “Commencement Date” means July 1, 2025.

1.2 “Term” means the period commencing on the Commencement Date and ending on June 30, 2030, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Lease.

1.3 “Base Rent” means the amount payable by the Tenant to the Landlord pursuant to Section 4 of this Lease.

1.4 “Premises” means the commercial unit located at 238 Commerce Way, Unit 8, Oakville, Ontario, comprising approximately 3,000 square feet of retail space.

2. LEASE AND PREMISES

2.1 The Landlord hereby leases to the Tenant, and the Tenant hereby rents from the Landlord, the Premises to be used solely for the operation of a retail outdoor equipment store and related activities consistent with normal retail operations.

2.2 The Tenant accepts the Premises in “as is” condition as of the date of execution of this Lease, subject to the Landlord completing minor repairs identified in Schedule “A” attached hereto, to be completed by no later than June 15, 2025.

3. TERM

3.1 The Term of this Lease shall be five (5) years commencing on the Commencement Date and expiring on June 30, 2030, unless earlier terminated pursuant to any provision of this Lease.

3.2 The Tenant shall have the option to renew this Lease for an additional period of five (5) years upon giving written notice to the Landlord not less than 180 days prior to the expiration of the Term, on terms and conditions to be mutually agreed upon.

4. RENT

4.1 Base Rent: The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord base rent in the amount of Twenty-Six Thousand Four Hundred Dollars ($26,400.00) annually, payable in equal monthly instalments of Two Thousand Two Hundred Dollars ($2,200.00) in advance on the first day of each calendar month without deduction or setoff.

4.2 Additional Rent: The Tenant shall also pay its proportionate share of all operating expenses, including property taxes, insurance, maintenance, and utilities, as detailed in Schedule “B” attached hereto, payable monthly in arrears based on the Tenant’s ratio of leased Premises area to total leasable area of the building.

5. USE AND COMPLIANCE

5.1 The Tenant agrees to use the Premises solely for the retail sale of outdoor recreational equipment, clothing, and accessories, and for no other purpose without prior written consent of the Landlord.

5.2 The Tenant shall at all times comply with all applicable federal, provincial, and municipal laws, statutes, bylaws, regulations, and orders relating to the use and occupancy of the Premises.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

6.1 The Landlord shall maintain and repair the structural components of the Premises, including roof, exterior walls, and foundation.

6.2 The Tenant shall keep the Premises and all interior improvements in good order, condition, and repair (reasonable wear and tear excepted), and shall promptly make all non-structural repairs and replacements.

7. NON-COMPETE COVENANT

7.1 For a period of three (3) years following the expiration or earlier termination of this Lease, the Landlord agrees that no business operating from the commercial complex located at 238 Commerce Way, Oakville, Ontario, or any property owned or controlled by the Landlord within a radius of five (5) kilometers from the Premises, shall directly or indirectly engage in the retail sale of outdoor recreational equipment, clothing, or accessories competitive with the Tenant’s business as conducted at the Premises.

7.2 The Landlord acknowledges and agrees that this covenant is reasonable, necessary for the protection of the Tenant’s goodwill, and enforceable under Ontario law.

7.3 In the event of breach of this covenant, the Landlord shall be liable to the Tenant for all damages sustained, including but not limited to lost profits, and injunctive relief may be sought by the Tenant.

8. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

8.1 The Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet the Premises or any part thereof without the prior written consent of the Landlord, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld.

9. INSURANCE

9.1 The Tenant shall, at its expense, maintain commercial general liability insurance in an amount not less than Five Million Dollars ($5,000,000) per occurrence, naming the Landlord as an additional insured.

9.2 The Tenant shall provide proof of such insurance to the Landlord prior to the Commencement Date and upon each renewal.

10. DEFAULT

10.1 If the Tenant fails to pay any rent or other amounts due hereunder within ten (10) days after written notice of non-payment, or otherwise defaults in any covenant hereof and fails to remedy the default within thirty (30) days after written notice thereof, the Landlord may terminate this Lease and re-enter the Premises.

10.2 If the Landlord breaches any material term or covenant of this Lease and fails to remedy same within thirty (30) days after written notice, the Tenant may pursue all remedies available at law or in equity.

11. TERMINATION

11.1 Notwithstanding any other provision, the Tenant may terminate this Lease after the third anniversary of the Commencement Date by giving the Landlord at least ninety (90) days’ prior written notice.

11.2 Upon expiration or earlier termination of this Lease, the Tenant shall surrender possession of the Premises in good condition, reasonable wear and tear excepted.

12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

12.1 This Lease and the rights and obligations of the parties hereunder shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Province of Ontario.

12.2 The parties hereby agree to submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Ontario Superior Court of Justice regarding any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Lease.
