COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made and entered into as of the 1st day of January, 2025, by and between Evergreen Property Holdings, LLC, a Washington limited liability company ("Landlord"), and Cascade Fresh Foods, Inc., a Washington corporation ("Tenant").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Landlord is the owner of certain commercial real property located at 1024 Pine Street, Suite 300, Seattle, King County, Washington 98101 (the "Premises");

WHEREAS, Tenant desires to lease the Premises from Landlord for the purpose of operating a specialty grocery store;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Premises" means the leased premises described above and any appurtenant areas assigned to Tenant as part of this Lease.

1.2 "Lease Term" means the period commencing on February 1, 2025, and ending on January 31, 2028, unless sooner terminated pursuant to this Lease.

1.3 "Base Rent" means the fixed monthly rent amount payable by Tenant as set forth in Section 3.

1.4 "Late Payment Penalty" means a charge of two percent (2%) of any unpaid rent amount per month, assessed on all amounts not received within five (5) days after the due date.

2. LEASE TERM

2.1 Term. Landlord hereby leases to Tenant, and Tenant hereby leases from Landlord, the Premises for a fixed term of three (3) years commencing on February 1, 2025 (the "Commencement Date") and ending at 11:59 p.m. on January 31, 2028 (the "Expiration Date").

2.2 Possession. Landlord shall deliver possession of the Premises to Tenant in broom-clean condition on the Commencement Date.

3. RENT AND PAYMENT TERMS

3.1 Base Rent. Tenant shall pay to Landlord monthly Base Rent in the amount of $8,500.00, payable in advance on or before the first (1st) day of each calendar month during the Lease Term.

3.2 Method of Payment. All rent payments shall be made by check, wire transfer, or electronic funds transfer to Landlord at the following address or account:

Evergreen Property Holdings, LLC
P.O. Box 8472
Seattle, WA 98104

or to such other place as Landlord may designate in writing.

3.3 Late Payment Penalty. If any rent or other payment due under this Lease is not received by Landlord within five (5) calendar days after the due date, Tenant shall pay, in addition to the rent due, a Late Payment Penalty equal to two percent (2%) of the delinquent amount for each month or portion thereof that such amount remains unpaid, commencing on the sixth (6th) calendar day after the due date.

3.4 No Waiver. Acceptance of a Late Payment Penalty by Landlord shall not be deemed a waiver of Tenant’s default in payment of rent or a waiver of any other rights or remedies Landlord may have.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT

4.1 Deposit Amount. Upon execution of this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $8,500.00 as a security deposit (the "Security Deposit") to secure Tenant’s performance of its obligations under this Lease.

4.2 Use and Return. Landlord may use all or any portion of the Security Deposit to cure Tenant’s defaults, including unpaid rent or damages beyond normal wear and tear. The Security Deposit, or any remaining balance thereof, shall be returned to Tenant within thirty (30) days after the Expiration Date, less any lawful deductions.

5. USE OF PREMISES

5.1 Permitted Use. Tenant shall use and occupy the Premises solely for operating a specialty grocery store and for no other purpose without Landlord’s prior written consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld.

5.2 Compliance with Laws. Tenant shall comply with all applicable laws, regulations, ordinances, and codes relating to Tenant's use and occupancy of the Premises.

6. MAINTENANCE, REPAIRS AND ALTERATIONS

6.1 Landlord’s Responsibilities. Landlord shall maintain the structural components of the building, including roof, foundation, and exterior walls, in good repair.

6.2 Tenant’s Responsibilities. Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises, including plumbing fixtures, electrical wiring, and HVAC equipment, in good order and repair, at Tenant’s sole cost and expense.

6.3 Alterations. Tenant shall not make any structural alterations or improvements to the Premises without Landlord’s prior written consent. Any approved alterations shall be performed in a good and workmanlike manner and shall become property of Landlord upon expiration or termination of this Lease unless otherwise agreed in writing.

7. INSURANCE AND INDEMNIFICATION

7.1 Insurance. Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with a minimum combined single limit of $1,000,000 per occurrence and property insurance covering Tenant’s personal property and leasehold improvements.

7.2 Indemnification. Tenant shall indemnify, defend, and hold Landlord harmless from and against any claims, liabilities, losses, or damages arising from Tenant’s use or occupancy of the Premises, except to the extent caused by Landlord’s gross negligence or willful misconduct.

8. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

8.1 Prohibition. Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any portion of the Premises without Landlord’s prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

8.2 Conditions. Any approved assignment or sublease shall be subject to all terms and conditions of this Lease.

9. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

9.1 Events of Default. The following shall constitute events of default ("Default") by Tenant:

(a) Failure to pay rent or other sums within ten (10) days after written notice;

(b) Failure to perform any other covenant or condition hereof within thirty (30) days after written notice;

(c) Bankruptcy or insolvency of Tenant.

9.2 Remedies. Upon Default, Landlord may:

(a) Terminate this Lease and recover possession;

(b) Recover all rent due and unpaid plus damages;

(c) Pursue any other remedies available at law or in equity.

10. SURRENDER OF PREMISES

Upon expiration or termination of this Lease, Tenant shall surrender the Premises in broom-clean condition, free of Tenant’s personal property, and in good repair, reasonable wear and tear excepted.

This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Washington. The parties agree that any disputes arising under this Lease shall be resolved in the Superior Court of King County, Washington. The parties hereby submit to the jurisdiction of said court.

13.1 Entire Agreement. This Lease contains the entire agreement of the parties with respect to the Premises and supersedes all prior negotiations, understandings, or agreements.

13.2 Amendments. No amendment or modification of this Lease shall be valid unless in writing signed by both parties.

13.3 Severability. If any provision of this Lease is invalid or unenforceable, the remainder of this Lease shall continue in full force and effect.
