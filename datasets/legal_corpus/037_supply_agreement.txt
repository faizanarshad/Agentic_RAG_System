RECITALS

WHEREAS, Buyer designs and sells advanced robotic systems and desires to purchase from Supplier certain custom electronic components for incorporation into its products; and

WHEREAS, Supplier is in the business of manufacturing and supplying such electronic components and desires to sell such components to Buyer under the terms and conditions set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1.1 “Products” means the custom electronic circuit boards and related components specified in Purchase Orders submitted by Buyer to Supplier pursuant to this Agreement.

1.3 “Specifications” means the technical requirements, designs, drawings, and related documentation provided by Buyer to Supplier describing the Products.

1.4 “Work Product” means all inventions, designs, developments, improvements, processes, computer programs, documentation, reports, drawings, and other works of authorship created, conceived, written, or otherwise developed by Supplier, alone or with others, in whole or in part, in connection with the performance of this Agreement, including all modifications and enhancements.

1.5 “Pre-Existing IP” means Supplier’s proprietary inventions, developments, technologies, software, tools, or other intellectual property that exist prior to the Effective Date or are developed independently of this Agreement.

2. SCOPE OF SUPPLY

2.1 Supply of Products. Supplier shall manufacture and supply to Buyer the Products in accordance with the Specifications, quantities, delivery schedules, and prices set forth in Purchase Orders issued during the Term.

2.2 Forecast and Minimum Orders. Buyer shall provide Supplier with a rolling forecast of Product quantities every quarter, but only Purchase Orders shall be binding. Buyer agrees to a minimum aggregate purchase quantity of 50,000 units over the Term.

2.3 Delivery and Acceptance. Supplier shall deliver Products FOB Buyer’s facility in Bellevue, WA, by the delivery dates specified in Purchase Orders. Buyer shall conduct inspection within five (5) business days of delivery and may reject nonconforming Products by written notice to Supplier.
