FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS

7. Under the terms of the Agreement, Johnson & Covert agreed to perform all construction work, including but not limited to structural framing, electrical wiring, HVAC installation, and interior finishing.  

8. The total contract price was $3,750,000.00, payable in installments tied to completion of specified milestones, with the final payment due upon substantial completion of the Project.  

9. The Agreement required Johnson & Covert to substantially complete the Project no later than October 31, 2026 ("Completion Deadline"), subject to certain permitted extensions for Force Majeure events or changes in the scope of work approved in writing by Oceanic Technologies.

13. Oceanic Technologies improperly withheld payment without providing any reasonable justification or invoking any contractual right to withhold payment.  

14. Additionally, in June 2026, Oceanic Technologies issued multiple unilateral change orders that significantly altered the Project scope without providing corresponding adjustments to time or compensation, in direct violation of the Agreement’s change order provisions.  

15. These unauthorized change orders caused delays and increased costs, which Plaintiff communicated to Defendant in writing on July 10, 2026, requesting a meeting to discuss equitable adjustments, but Defendant failed or refused to engage in good faith negotiations.
