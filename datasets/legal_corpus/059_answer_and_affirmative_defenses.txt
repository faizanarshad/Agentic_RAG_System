4.3 In response to paragraph 9, Centaur admits delivery of partial Deliverables on 15 January 2027 but denies that such partial delivery constituted a breach or that Phoenix Tech suffered any loss.  

5. Affirmative Defences  

5.1 Set-Off and Counterclaim  

5.1.1 Phoenix Tech materially breached its obligations under clause 6 of the Agreement by failing to provide essential technical specifications and timely approvals required for Centaur’s development work.  

5.1.2 As a result of Phoenix Tech’s breaches, Centaur incurred additional costs amounting to £142,500, which remain unpaid. Centaur therefore makes a counterclaim for damages in this amount.  

5.2 Force Majeure  

5.2.1 Centaur relies on clause 12 of the Agreement which provides for relief from performance due to force majeure events.  

5.2.2 Between 1 November 2026 and 15 December 2026, Centaur’s development team was impacted by a COVID-19 outbreak causing a temporary closure of its London office and remote working restrictions which substantially impaired its ability to perform.  

5.2.3 Centaur gave timely notice of this force majeure event to Phoenix Tech on 3 November 2026, in accordance with the Agreement.  

5.2.4 Centaur is therefore excused from non-performance or delay during the force majeure period.  

5.3 Waiver and Estoppel  

5.3.1 By conduct and correspondence, including emails dated 12 September 2026, 15 October 2026, and 18 December 2026, Phoenix Tech waived strict compliance with the Completion Date and accepted late deliveries without objection.  

5.3.2 Phoenix Tech is estopped from now asserting breach based on timelines.  

5.4 Failure to Mitigate Loss  

5.4.1 Phoenix Tech failed to mitigate its alleged loss, including failing to continue using partial Deliverables or procure alternative solutions despite knowledge of delays.  

5.4.2 Such failure precludes or reduces any damages claimable.
