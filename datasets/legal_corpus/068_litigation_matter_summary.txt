Litigation Matter Summary: Sterling Innovations Inc. v. Vanguard Technologies Ltd.

1. Introduction

1.1. This Litigation Matter Summary ("Summary") concerns the civil action commenced by Sterling Innovations Inc. ("Sterling"), a corporation incorporated under the laws of Ontario, against Vanguard Technologies Ltd. ("Vanguard"), likewise incorporated in Ontario. The matter is currently before the Ontario Superior Court of Justice under court file number CV-25-3456-00CP.

1.2. The action was initiated on February 12, 2025, alleging breach of contract and misappropriation of confidential information.

2. Parties

2.1. Plaintiff: Sterling Innovations Inc., headquartered at 145 Maple Grove Avenue, Toronto, Ontario, M5J 2K8, engaged in research and development of advanced renewable energy technologies.

2.2. Defendant: Vanguard Technologies Ltd., located at 89 Wellington Street East, Toronto, Ontario, M5A 1E3, specializing in software development and technology consulting services.

3. Background and Factual Summary

3.1. On August 15, 2023, Sterling and Vanguard entered into a Confidential Consulting Services Agreement ("Consulting Agreement"), pursuant to which Vanguard was retained to develop proprietary software to optimize Sterling's patented solar panel management system.

3.2. The Consulting Agreement included confidentiality provisions and a clause restricting Vanguard from using Sterling’s confidential information beyond the purposes expressly authorized.

3.3. Sterling alleges that in the period between January and May 2024, Vanguard improperly accessed and used Sterling’s trade secrets and confidential technical data, subsequently incorporating these in its own competing software product, "Vanguard SolarSuite," launched publicly in November 2024.

3.4. Sterling contends that Vanguard’s actions constitute a breach of the Consulting Agreement and wrongful misappropriation under Ontario’s trade secrets law.

4. Procedural History

4.1. The Statement of Claim was filed on February 12, 2025.

4.2. Vanguard filed a Statement of Defence and Counterclaim on March 28, 2025, denying the allegations and alleging Sterling failed to make timely payments under the Consulting Agreement.

4.3. Discovery was conducted between October 2025 and January 2026, with significant document production and examinations for discovery of corporate representatives.

4.4. Mediation was ordered by the Court and took place on March 10, 2026, but did not result in settlement.

4.5. Trial was scheduled to commence on June 15, 2026.

5. Settlement Negotiations and Resolution

5.1. On May 20, 2026, prior to trial commencement, the parties resumed settlement discussions.

5.2. On June 2, 2026, the parties reached a confidential settlement agreement ("Settlement Agreement").

5.3. The Settlement Agreement provides, inter alia, for:

   5.3.1. Vanguard to pay Sterling a lump sum of CAD 2,500,000 (two million five hundred thousand Canadian dollars) within 30 days of the Settlement Agreement's execution.

   5.3.2. Mutual release of all claims related to the dispute.

   5.3.3. A non-disparagement clause preventing either party, their officers, directors, employees, or agents from making any public statements or communications that could reasonably be construed as disparaging or damaging to the other party’s reputation or business interests.

   5.3.4. Confidentiality obligations regarding the terms and existence of the Settlement Agreement.

5.4. The Settlement Agreement was executed on June 10, 2026.

6. Obligations and Deadlines under the Settlement Agreement

6.1. Payment: Vanguard is obligated to remit the settlement payment of CAD 2,500,000 to Sterling’s designated trust account no later than July 10, 2026.

6.2. Confidentiality: Both parties are required to maintain strict confidentiality regarding the terms and existence of the Settlement Agreement indefinitely, except as required by law or with mutual written consent.

6.3. Non-Disparagement: Neither party nor their affiliates may make any disparaging remarks about the other, either publicly or privately, commencing on the date the Settlement Agreement is signed and continuing for a period of five (5) years.

6.4. Enforcement: Breach of any settlement term will entitle the non-breaching party to seek specific performance, damages, or other appropriate remedies.

7. Current Status and Next Steps

7.1. As of February 25, 2027, the settlement payment was received and confirmed by Sterling’s legal counsel.

7.2. The Court was notified on July 15, 2026, that the matter had been resolved and the action was formally dismissed on consent.

7.3. Both parties have complied with their initial obligations under the Settlement Agreement.

7.4. Monitoring remains in place to ensure ongoing compliance with confidentiality and non-disparagement provisions.
