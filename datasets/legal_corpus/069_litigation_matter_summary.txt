1. Parties  
Plaintiff: GreenTech Innovations, Inc. ("GreenTech")  
Defendant: Apex Manufacturing Solutions, LLC ("Apex")  

2. Nature of the Case  
This matter involves a breach of contract dispute arising out of an agreement dated March 12, 2024, between GreenTech and Apex for the design, development, and delivery of custom industrial battery packs for GreenTech's electric vehicle prototypes. GreenTech alleges that Apex failed to deliver conforming products on schedule and that the delivered units were defective, causing substantial financial damages. Apex denies liability and contends that GreenTech wrongfully terminated the contract.  

3. Relevant Jurisdiction and Governing Law  
The parties' contract specifies Illinois law as the governing law. The case was originally filed in the Circuit Court of Cook County, Illinois, under Case No. 2025-CV-09876.  

4. Procedural History  
The Complaint was filed by GreenTech on January 15, 2025. Apex filed its Answer and Counterclaim on March 1, 2025. The Counterclaim alleges breach of contract by GreenTech for wrongful termination and failure to pay agreed milestones.  

Following extensive discovery, both parties filed summary judgment motions. On November 10, 2026, the Circuit Court issued a written Order granting summary judgment to GreenTech on liability as to Apex’s breach of contract and denying Apex’s counterclaims. The Court further awarded GreenTech damages in the amount of $2,150,000, representing lost profits and costs related to replacing the defective battery packs. Apex timely filed a Notice of Appeal on November 30, 2026.  

5. Key Contractual Terms  
a) Contract Date: March 12, 2024  
b) Contract Value: $5,000,000  
c) Delivery Schedule: Initial prototype battery packs due by September 30, 2024; final production units due by March 31, 2025.  
d) Payment Terms: 30% upfront ($1,500,000) upon contract execution; 40% ($2,000,000) upon prototype delivery acceptance; 30% ($1,500,000) upon final delivery acceptance.  
e) Termination Clause: Parties may terminate for material breach upon 30 days’ written notice and an opportunity to cure.  

6. Factual Background  
GreenTech entered into the contract with Apex to produce battery packs designed specifically for its electric vehicle line. Apex received the upfront payment of $1,500,000 shortly after contract execution. Apex delivered the prototype battery packs on October 15, 2024, but GreenTech rejected them citing numerous technical defects, including failure to meet voltage and durability specifications outlined in Section 3.2 of the contract. Apex disputed the claims and proposed remediation.  

GreenTech issued a formal cure notice on November 20, 2024, requiring Apex to remedy the defects within 30 days. Apex failed to complete the necessary adjustments by December 20, 2024. Subsequently, GreenTech terminated the contract on January 5, 2025, after which Apex ceased work.  

GreenTech contracted a third party to produce replacement battery packs at a cost exceeding $3,000,000. GreenTech claims these costs plus lost profits due to production delays totaling $1,000,000, resulting in its damages claim of $2,150,000 after accounting for partial payments and mitigation.  

7. Apex’s Position  
Apex asserts that GreenTech improperly rejected the prototype battery packs, which met contract specifications when tested independently. Apex contends GreenTech breached the contract first by refusing to cooperate in the cure process and unilaterally terminating the contract without cause. Apex’s counterclaim seeks damages of $1,200,000 based on unpaid milestones and lost future profits.  

8. Trial Court’s Summary Judgment Decision (November 10, 2026)  
The Circuit Court analyzed the contract terms, expert reports, and deposition testimony. It found that GreenTech’s rejection of the prototypes was justified due to material defects and that Apex failed to cure in the required timeframe. The Court held that GreenTech was entitled to terminate the contract.  

The Court entered judgment awarding GreenTech $2,150,000 in damages and dismissed Apex’s counterclaim. Apex filed a timely appeal pursuant to Illinois Supreme Court Rules 301 and 303.  

9. Pending Appeal (Appellate Case No. 1-26-1478)  
Apex’s Notice of Appeal was docketed in the Illinois First District Appellate Court on November 30, 2026. Apex’s opening brief was filed on February 20, 2027, challenging the trial court’s findings on contract breach, cure, and damages calculations.  

GreenTech’s Answer Brief was filed on April 15, 2027. The parties have completed briefing, and oral argument is scheduled for June 10, 2027.  

Key issues on appeal include:  
- Whether GreenTech properly rejected the prototype battery packs under the contract’s acceptance criteria.  
- Whether Apex was afforded a reasonable opportunity to cure.  
- Accuracy of the damages awarded, including the calculation of lost profits and consequential damages.  
- Dismissal of Apex’s counterclaims.
