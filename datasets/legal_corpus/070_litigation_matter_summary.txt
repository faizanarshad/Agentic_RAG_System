III. Nature of the Litigation  
The action involves claims brought by GreenTech against Apex alleging breach of contract, specifically related to the alleged failure of Apex to deliver custom-manufactured components critical to GreenTech’s production line, as specified under the Supply and Manufacturing Agreement dated January 15, 2025 (“Agreement”).  

IV. Background and Factual Summary  
1. On January 15, 2025, GreenTech Innovations, Inc. and Apex Manufacturing Solutions, LLC entered into the Agreement whereby Apex agreed to design, manufacture, and deliver custom microchip encapsulation units to GreenTech.  
2. Per the Agreement, Apex was to deliver an initial order of 10,000 units by July 15, 2025, with subsequent monthly deliveries of 5,000 units each through December 31, 2025. The total contract price was $3,750,000, to be paid in installments based on delivery milestones.  
3. GreenTech alleges that Apex failed to meet the initial delivery deadline of July 15, 2025, and subsequently delayed or failed to supply the monthly deliveries as required.  
4. GreenTech further claims that Apex’s quality control defects in units delivered in August and September 2025 rendered those units unusable, leading to production shutdowns and financial losses estimated at $1,200,000.  
5. Apex counters that delays resulted from supply chain disruptions beyond its control and maintains that units delivered met contractual specifications. Apex also contends that GreenTech failed to provide timely payment for delivered units, constituting a material breach.  

V. Procedural History
