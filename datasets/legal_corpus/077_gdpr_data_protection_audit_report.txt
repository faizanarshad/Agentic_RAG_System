1. Introduction and Scope

1.1. This report presents the findings of the GDPR Data Protection Compliance Audit conducted by Silvergate Data Privacy Consultants LLC (“Auditor”) on behalf of VenariTech Solutions, Inc. (“VenariTech” or “Company”) pursuant to the Data Processing Compliance Agreement dated December 15, 2024. The audit scope covered VenariTech’s processing activities related to the personal data of data subjects located in the European Union as defined under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”).

1.2. The GDPR, while a European Union regulation, applies extraterritorially to companies such as VenariTech operating within the State of California and processing the personal data of EU residents. This audit was conducted under the California Privacy Enforcement Act (CPEA) supplemented by GDPR guidance, effective as of January 1, 2025.

1.3. The audit period covered was July 1, 2024 through December 31, 2024. The field audit took place on January 15-17, 2025, including interviews with the Company’s Data Protection Officer (DPO) Mr. Sebastian Cole and relevant department heads.

2. Definitions

2.1. “Personal Data” means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (Data Subject) as defined under Article 4(1) GDPR.

2.2. “Processing” means any operation or set of operations performed on Personal Data as defined under Article 4(2) GDPR.

2.3. “Controller” means VenariTech acting as the entity determining the purposes and means of Processing under Article 4(7) GDPR.

2.4. “Processor” includes any party processing data on behalf of the Controller.

2.5. “Data Subject Rights” refers to the rights granted to individuals under GDPR Chapter III, including the right of access, rectification, and erasure.

3. Executive Summary

3.1. VenariTech has demonstrated an overall commitment to GDPR compliance and has enacted numerous policies and technical safeguards aligned with GDPR principles. The Company has appointed a qualified DPO and established training programs for staff.

3.2. However, the audit identified three critical non-compliance findings requiring urgent remediation to avoid potential administrative fines up to €20,000,000 or 4% of global annual turnover, in accordance with Article 83 GDPR and supplementary California enforcement provisions.

3.3. These critical findings pertain to: inadequate data subject consent mechanisms (Section 5.2), deficient records of processing activities (Section 5.3), and insufficient data breach notification procedures (Section 5.4).

4. Audit Methodology

4.1. The audit methodology included:  
- Documentation review of privacy policies, processing records, contracts, and security protocols.  
- On-site verification of technical and organizational measures.  
- Interviews with key personnel including DPO, IT security leads, and Legal Counsel.  
- Sample-based testing of data subject requests and consent records.

4.2. Compliance criteria were established according to GDPR Articles 5, 6, 7, 12-22, 30, 32, 33, and 34, as well as CPEA Sections 1798.100 et seq.

5. Detailed Findings and Recommendations

5.1. Finding 1: Inadequate Data Subject Consent Mechanisms (Critical)  
5.1.1. VenariTech employs digital opt-in forms for marketing communications but the audit revealed a lack of granular consent choices, combining multiple purposes into a single consent checkbox. This approach violates GDPR Article 7(2) requiring freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent.  
5.1.2. Additionally, mechanisms do not allow easy withdrawal of consent, impeding Data Subject rights under Article 7(3).  
5.1.3. Recommendation: VenariTech must redesign consent workflows to include separate checkboxes for each processing purpose and implement a user-friendly, accessible method for withdrawing consent. All changes must be audited and documented within the internal Consent Management System (CMS).  
5.1.4. Remediation Deadline: June 30, 2025.

5.2. Finding 2: Deficient Records of Processing Activities (RoPA) (Critical)  
5.2.1. Per Article 30 GDPR, the Controller is required to maintain comprehensive records of processing activities. Auditors found VenariTech’s RoPA documents incomplete, lacking details on data categories processed, data sharing recipients, and retention periods for multiple processing operations.  
5.2.2. Several processing activities performed by VenariTech’s marketing department and customer support lack documentation entirely.  
5.2.3. Recommendation: VenariTech must update and complete their RoPA records to comprehensively capture all processing operations, incorporating data flow diagrams and categorizing data recipients. The updated RoPA should be reviewed and approved by the DPO and Legal Counsel.  
5.2.4. Remediation Deadline: September 30, 2025.

5.3. Finding 3: Insufficient Data Breach Notification Procedures (Critical)  
5.3.1. While VenariTech maintains an internal security incident response plan, the audit revealed gaps in breach identification and notification timelines. Particularly, no formal procedure exists to ensure notification to supervisory authorities within the mandatory 72 hours per Article 33(1) GDPR.  
5.3.2. Testing of the log and reporting system found delays exceeding 96 hours between breach detection and escalation.  
5.3.3. Recommendation: Immediate enhancement of incident response protocols is required, including automated alert systems, defined escalation workflows, and staff training emphasizing statutory notification deadlines. VenariTech should also establish a breach notification template compliant with Article 33 and 34 GDPR requirements.  
5.3.4. Remediation Deadline: April 30, 2025.

6. Additional Observations (Non-critical)

6.1. Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) Handling: VenariTech’s average response time to DSARs was 45 days, exceeding the regulatory requirement of one month (Article 12(3) GDPR). While not critical, process improvements are recommended.

6.2. Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA): The Company has conducted DPIAs for major projects but several newly launched services lack documented DPIAs. Compliance with Article 35 GDPR should be enforced.

7. Conclusion

7.1. VenariTech Solutions, Inc. has made significant strides toward GDPR alignment but must urgently address the critical areas highlighted above to ensure full compliance and mitigate potential enforcement actions.

7.2. The Auditor will conduct a follow-up review after the remediation deadlines to verify corrective measures.

7.3. Failure to comply with these recommendations within the specified timelines may result in referral to the California Privacy Enforcement Board and potential administrative sanctions.
