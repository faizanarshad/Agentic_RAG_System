HIPAA Security Risk Assessment and Compliance Record  
AvanteHealth Medical Group  
Conducted for the Period: January 2025 – December 2026  
Jurisdiction: State of New York  

1. Introduction  
This Security Risk Assessment and Compliance Record (“Assessment”) has been prepared for AvanteHealth Medical Group (“AvanteHealth” or “the Covered Entity”), pursuant to the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (“HIPAA”) Security Rule, 45 C.F.R. § 164.308, and the applicable laws of the State of New York. This Assessment covers all electronic protected health information (ePHI) systems and processes managed or operated by AvanteHealth within the State of New York for the period January 1, 2025 through December 31, 2026.  

2. Purpose  
The primary purpose of this Assessment is to identify security risks and vulnerabilities affecting AvanteHealth’s ePHI, evaluate existing administrative, physical, and technical safeguards, and establish a remediation roadmap to ensure ongoing HIPAA Security Rule compliance. This Assessment also addresses overdue remediation items identified in a prior audit conducted in March 2024.  

3. Scope  
3.1 Covered Systems: Electronic Health Record (EHR) System — AvanteHealth EHR v4.9; Patient Portal; Internal Network Infrastructure; and Third-Party Cloud Storage Provider (SecureDataCloud, Inc.).  
3.2 Locations: AvanteHealth main office (1204 Madison Avenue, Albany, NY) and remote telehealth sites.  
3.3 Personnel: All clinical, administrative, and IT staff with access to ePHI.  

4. Previous Audit Findings and Overdue Remediation  
On March 15, 2024, an external audit was conducted by SecureCom Consulting, LLC (Audit Report No. SC-2024-03-AH). The audit identified several critical findings requiring remediation by September 15, 2024. As of December 31, 2024, two remediation items remained outstanding:  
4.1 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Implementation: The audit recommended full MFA deployment for all systems accessing ePHI. Partial implementation occurred, but MFA remains disabled for remote telehealth staff.  
4.2 Risk Analysis Documentation: The prior risk analysis was deemed incomplete, lacking sufficient threat modeling and risk prioritization documentation.  

Failure to remediate these items violates 45 C.F.R. § 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(B) and exposes AvanteHealth to liability under New York State Public Health Law § 499-c.  

5. Current Risk Assessment Methodology  
5.1 Risk Identification: Review of system architecture, access controls, security incident logs (January 2025–July 2026), employee interviews, and policy documents.  
5.2 Risk Analysis: Evaluation of likelihood and impact of identified threats on confidentiality, integrity, and availability of ePHI, using a qualitative risk matrix.  
5.3 Risk Mitigation Planning: Development of remedial actions prioritized by risk severity and resource availability.  

6. Findings and Risk Analysis  
6.1 Access Control and Authentication  
6.1.1 Risk: MFA is not fully implemented for remote telehealth personnel, which exposes ePHI to unauthorized access through compromised credentials.  
6.1.2 Likelihood: High  
6.1.3 Impact: Severe — potential breach involving patient records could lead to regulatory penalties and reputational harm.  
6.2 Risk Status: Overdue since prior audit (expected remediation by 09/15/2024).  

6.2 Network Security  
6.2.1 Risk: Firewall configurations lack segmentation between clinical systems and administrative networks, increasing the attack surface.  
6.2.2 Likelihood: Moderate  
6.2.3 Impact: High — possible lateral movement by threat actors could lead to large-scale data exposure.  
6.3 Risk Status: New finding.  

6.3 Risk Analysis Documentation  
6.3.1 Risk: Prior risk analysis documentation remains incomplete, with inadequate threat models and insufficient prioritization of risks.  
6.3.2 Likelihood: N/A (Documentation deficiency)  
6.3.3 Impact: Moderate — impedes effective remediation planning and compliance reporting.  
6.4 Risk Status: Overdue since prior audit.  

6.4 Data Backup and Recovery  
6.4.1 Risk: Backup schedules do not align with current data volumes, leading to potential loss of recent ePHI in case of system failure.  
6.4.2 Likelihood: Moderate  
6.4.3 Impact: High — loss of patient data may disrupt care delivery and violate HIPAA data integrity requirements.  
6.5 Risk Status: New finding.
