Data Breach Incident Report  
Compliance Record No. CBIR-CA-2025-0427

1. Introduction  
This Data Breach Incident Report (“Report”) is prepared pursuant to the requirements under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and related state data protection laws. This Report documents the breach event involving Verdant Health Systems, Inc. (“Verdant Health” or the “Company”), a California-based healthcare software provider, and provides critical findings, remedial measures, and compliance obligations as of the date of this document.

2. Incident Overview  
2.1 Date of Incident Discovery: February 15, 2025  
2.2 Date of Incident Occurrence: Between January 20 and January 27, 2025  
2.3 Reporting Date: April 27, 2025  
2.4 Incident Identification Number: VH-CB-2025-001  
2.5 Location: Verdant Health Systems Headquarters, San Jose, California  
2.6 Nature of Data Compromised: Personally Identifiable Information (PII), including full names, dates of birth, social security numbers, health insurance information, and limited health records of approximately 87,000 patients and 5,400 employees.  
2.7 Vectors of Breach: Unauthorized external intrusion via exploitation of an unpatched vulnerability in Verdant Health’s cloud infrastructure system.

3. Executive Summary  
On or about January 20, 2025, an unauthorized third party gained access to Verdant Health systems through a previously undisclosed vulnerability in a third-party cloud service utilized by the Company. The breach was discovered on February 15, 2025, following abnormal network activity alerts. The intruder had exfiltrated confidential patient and employee PII during the one-week window. Verdant Health immediately initiated containment measures, engaged a cybersecurity incident response team, and notified appropriate California authorities and affected individuals in accord with governing laws.

4. Detailed Findings  
4.1 Finding 1: Insufficient Patch Management and Vulnerability Assessment  
The cybersecurity review found that Verdant Health’s IT department had failed to apply a critical security patch released by the cloud infrastructure provider (CypherCloud Services) in December 2024. This failure directly enabled the attackers to exploit the vulnerability. Internal controls for vulnerability assessment and patch management were inadequate in frequency and scope.  
4.2 Finding 2: Incomplete Data Encryption Practices  
Despite the sensitivity of the data stored, the Company’s encryption protocols were only applied to data at rest within on-premises servers. Data stored or transmitted within the cloud infrastructure remained only partially encrypted, including sensitive fields such as social security numbers and health insurance IDs, creating exposure during the breach.  
4.3 Finding 3: Ineffective Incident Response and Detection Mechanisms  
The breach went undetected for approximately 26 days, highlighting significant shortcomings in Verdant Health’s intrusion detection systems (IDS) and incident response plan. Network monitoring tools lacked real-time anomaly detection and logs were inadequately maintained, delaying identification and mitigation of the breach.

5. Legal and Regulatory Implications  
As a California-based entity processing sensitive personal data, Verdant Health is subject to the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), and Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) regulations. The breach exposes Verdant Health to potential administrative enforcement actions, civil penalties, and private right of action lawsuits. The Company has voluntarily reported the incident to the California Attorney General’s Office under case number AGC-2025-1982.

6. Required Remediation and Compliance Deadlines  
In alignment with California state law requirements and best practices, the following critical remediation steps have been mandated with firm deadlines:

6.1 Remediation 1: Comprehensive Patch Management Program Enhancement  
Verdant Health shall develop and implement an enhanced patch management protocol that includes automatic patch deployment, bi-weekly vulnerability assessments, and third-party audit verification. This program must be fully operational no later than July 1, 2025. Certification of completion must be submitted to the California Data Protection Authority (CDPA) by July 15, 2025.

6.2 Remediation 2: Full End-to-End Encryption Deployment for Data at Rest and in Transit  
The Company must encrypt all sensitive PII both at rest and in transit, including within cloud infrastructure environments. This encryption must meet or exceed AES-256 standards and be verified via an independent security assessment. Implementation must be completed by September 30, 2025, with an attestation report submitted to the CDPA no later than October 15, 2025.

6.3 Remediation 3: Incident Detection and Response Revamp  
Verdant Health is obligated to overhaul its incident response framework by deploying advanced real-time intrusion detection systems, instituting mandatory incident response drills every quarter, and maintaining detailed, immutable audit logs. A formal incident response plan revision and proof of deployment of new detection tools must be presented to the CDPA by December 31, 2025.

7. Notification and Victim Support  
7.1 Notification Letters  
In compliance with Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.82, Verdant Health has issued written notifications to all affected individuals by March 3, 2025. The notifications included information on the nature of the breach, steps taken by the Company, and recommended precautions for victims.  
7.2 Credit Monitoring Services  
Verdant Health has offered twelve (12) months of free credit monitoring and identity theft protection services to all affected individuals, activation available upon receipt of notification. Program enrollment will be monitored quarterly, with quarterly status reports due to the CDPA.

8. Financial Impact and Liability Assessment  
The preliminary estimated financial impact from the breach, including forensic investigation, legal fees, notification costs, credit monitoring, and regulatory fines, is projected to be approximately $15.8 million USD. This amount excludes potential class action lawsuit settlements and reputational damage costs, which remain to be quantified.

9. Governance and Accountability  
Mr. Evan M. Thurston, Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) of Verdant Health, has assumed full responsibility for remediation oversight and compliance reporting. The Company’s Board of Directors convened an emergency meeting on February 20, 2025, to address governance failures and has mandated quarterly oversight reports to the CEO and CDPA.

10. Conclusion  
This Report represents a full disclosure of the breach incident at Verdant Health Systems, Inc. and a commitment to corrective action in compliance with California law. The Company recognizes the severity of the breach and is dedicated to restoring data security and public trust.
