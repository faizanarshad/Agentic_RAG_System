DATA BREACH INCIDENT REPORT  
Compliance Record No. CR-IL-2027-0423  

Issued by: Illinois Department of Cybersecurity and Data Protection (IDCDP)  
Date of Report: April 23, 2027  

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------  
1. Introduction  

This Data Breach Incident Report ("Report") is issued pursuant to the Illinois Personal Information Protection Act (815 ILCS 530) and the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (740 ILCS 14), following the discovery of a data breach affecting the confidential personal information of Illinois residents maintained by Foresight Financial Technologies, Inc. ("Foresight" or the "Company").  

Foresight is a privately-held financial software company headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, providing cloud-based investment management solutions to retail and institutional clients.  

The breach was discovered on March 15, 2027, and is the subject of this formal compliance report due to overdue remediation identified in a prior audit from Q4 2025.  

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------  
2. Executive Summary  

On March 15, 2027, Foresight Financial Technologies experienced a data breach resulting from unauthorized access to its client information database. The breach exposed personally identifiable information ("PII") of approximately 18,750 Illinois residents, including names, addresses, social security numbers, dates of birth, and limited financial account information.  

An internal audit conducted by the independent firm ClearPath Security in November 2025 had previously identified critical vulnerabilities in Foresight’s database encryption protocols and network segmentation. Despite clear remediation directives issued with deadlines in January 2026, Foresight failed to implement the required security measures within the stipulated timeframe, resulting in overdue remediation.  

This report presents the breach timeline, impact analysis, legal obligations, and the corrective measures mandated by the Illinois Department of Cybersecurity and Data Protection ("IDCDP").  

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------  
3. Definitions  

3.1 "Breach" means the unauthorized acquisition, access, use, or disclosure of personal information maintained by Foresight that compromises the security, confidentiality, or integrity of such information.  

3.2 "Personal Information" or "PII" means any information that can be used to identify an individual, including but not limited to full name, social security number, date of birth, financial account numbers, and addresses.  

3.3 "Overdue Remediation" refers to failure by Foresight to complete corrective actions required by the November 2025 security audit by the January 31, 2026 deadline.  

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------  
4. Incident Description and Timeline  

4.1 Prior Audit Findings (November 2025)  
On November 12, 2025, ClearPath Security completed an audit of Foresight’s information systems. Key findings included inadequate encryption of PII stored in database backups, lack of multi-factor authentication (MFA) on privileged accounts, and insufficient network segmentation that allowed lateral movement within the system.  

4.2 Remediation Order (January 15, 2026)  
Following the audit, Foresight received a formal remediation order from IDCDP on January 15, 2026, mandating corrective actions, including:  

- Full implementation of AES-256 encryption for all PII in storage and backups.  
- Deployment of MFA for all privileged user accounts.  
- Establishment of network segmentation to isolate sensitive data environments.  

Deadline for full compliance was January 31, 2026.  

4.3 Failure to Remediate (January 31, 2026 – March 14, 2027)  
Foresight submitted a partial compliance report dated February 1, 2026, indicating partial encryption implementation but did not complete MFA rollout or network segmentation. Multiple follow-up notices from IDCDP between March and December 2026 demanded progress updates; however, Foresight failed to meet key milestones and provided inconsistent status reports.  

4.4 Breach Discovery (March 15, 2027)  
On March 15, 2027, Foresight’s Security Operations Center detected anomalous activity involving unauthorized queries executed against the client information database. Initial investigation revealed an external threat actor exploited an unpatched vulnerability in the web application firewall, gaining access to PII stored without proper encryption and with unsegmented network exposure.  

4.5 Breach Containment and Notification (March 16–22, 2027)  
Foresight immediately initiated containment procedures, including disabling compromised accounts, applying firewall patches, and initiating a mandatory password reset for all users. The Company notified IDCDP within 24 hours per 815 ILCS 530/10. Notifications to affected Illinois residents commenced on March 20, 2027.  

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------  
5. Breach Impact  

5.1 Data Compromised  
The affected dataset included:  

- Full names and dates of birth of 18,750 Illinois residents.  
- Social Security numbers for 18,500 individuals.  
- Account numbers and limited transaction histories for 12,300 clients.  
- Residential and mailing addresses.  

No biometric data was affected.  

5.2 Potential Harm  
Exposure of social security numbers and financial account information poses a risk of identity theft, financial fraud, and phishing attacks. The IDCDP estimates the risk level as high given the sensitivity and scope of exposed data.  

5.3 Estimated Financial Loss  
Foresight has preliminarily estimated direct financial losses and incident management costs at approximately $3.7 million. This figure includes forensic investigation fees, customer notification and credit monitoring services, legal expenses, and initial fines anticipated from regulatory penalties.  

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------  
6. Legal and Regulatory Considerations  

6.1 Governing Law  
This incident is governed under the Illinois Personal Information Protection Act (815 ILCS 530), requiring prompt notification and data security measures, and the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (740 ILCS 14) for any potential biometric data exposure (none in this case).  

6.2 Prior Enforcement and Overdue Remediation  
The overdue remediation of critical security vulnerabilities constitutes a compliance violation under 815 ILCS 530/15, which requires reasonable security measures to protect personal data. Foresight’s failure to meet remediation deadlines exacerbates liability and damages the Company’s defense in any enforcement proceedings.  

6.3 Reporting Obligations  
Under 815 ILCS 530/10, Foresight timely notified IDCDP and affected individuals within 72 hours of breach discovery, consistent with statutory requirements. However, failure to timely remediate prior audit findings remains a critical enforcement issue.  

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------  
7. Corrective Action Plan Required  

7.1 Immediate Actions (Deadline: May 15, 2027)  
Foresight must submit a detailed corrective action plan addressing overdue 2025 audit remediation items and breach response enhancements, including but not limited to:  

- Completion of AES-256 encryption across all PII data stores and backups.  
- Full deployment of MFA on all user accounts with elevated privileges.  
- Implementation of network segmentation isolating sensitive data environments.  
- Installation of automated patch management protocols for all web-facing infrastructure.  
- Comprehensive employee cybersecurity training schedule for 2027.  
- Engagement of third-party independent security audit to validate remedial measures.  

7.2 Long-Term Compliance Monitoring (Duration: 24 months)  
Foresight shall agree to quarterly reporting to IDCDP on remediation progress and cooperate with any required onsite audits or compliance reviews through April 2029.  

7.3 Financial Penalties  
Given the breach scope and overdue remediation, IDCDP intends to impose a civil penalty of $1.25 million pursuant to 815 ILCS 530/20, payable within 60 days of final administrative order.  

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------  
8. Conclusion  

The Foresight Financial Technologies data breach of March 15, 2027, underscores significant systemic failures in the Company’s cybersecurity posture, notably overdue remediation of known vulnerabilities identified over 15 months prior. The IDCDP mandates immediate and comprehensive remediation efforts and ongoing compliance monitoring to protect Illinois residents’ personal information and uphold statutory data protection standards.
