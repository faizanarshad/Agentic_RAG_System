DURABLE POWER OF ATTORNEY FOR FINANCIAL MATTERS

This Durable Power of Attorney (“Power of Attorney”) is made on this 15th day of March, 2025, by Eleanor M. Prescott, residing at 1423 Maplewood Drive, Seattle, Washington 98109 (“Principal”), pursuant to and in accordance with the laws of the State of Washington.

1. APPOINTMENT OF ATTORNEY-IN-FACT  
The Principal hereby appoints Gregory T. Mason, whose address is 5782 Elmview Lane, Tacoma, Washington 98408 (“Attorney-in-Fact”), as the Principal’s true and lawful attorney-in-fact to act in the name, place, and stead of the Principal with respect to the powers and authorities granted herein.

2. DURABILITY  
This Power of Attorney shall not be affected by the Principal’s subsequent disability, incapacity, or incompetence, and shall remain in full force and effect until revoked pursuant to Section 10.

3. DEFINITIONS  
For purposes of this Power of Attorney, the following definitions shall apply:  
a. “Financial Institution” shall mean any bank, savings association, credit union, or brokerage firm.  
b. “Property” shall mean all real and personal property wherever located, including tangible and intangible assets, financial accounts, business interests, and any other instruments of ownership.

4. GRANT OF GENERAL AUTHORITY  
Subject to the limitations stated in this Power of Attorney, and to the extent permitted by law, the Attorney-in-Fact shall have full power and authority to act on behalf of the Principal in all matters relating to the Principal’s financial and property affairs, including but not limited to the following powers:

4.1 Real Property Transactions  
To sell, lease, mortgage, exchange, or otherwise manage any real property owned by the Principal, including the execution of deeds, leases, contracts, and any other documents necessary to effectuate such transactions.

4.2 Banking Transactions  
To conduct all banking transactions, including opening and closing accounts, endorsing checks, withdrawing or depositing funds, and accessing safe deposit boxes.

4.3 Investment Powers  
To buy, sell, exchange, invest, reinvest, and manage stocks, bonds, mutual funds, or other investment assets of the Principal, and to exercise all voting rights and powers of management related thereto.

4.4 Business Operations  
To operate, buy, sell, or dissolve any business interests owned by the Principal, enter contracts, hire and fire employees, and sign any documents necessary to carry on the business.

4.5 Tax Matters  
To prepare, sign, and file federal, state, and local tax returns, pay taxes due, and represent the Principal before any taxation authority, including the Washington Department of Revenue and the Internal Revenue Service.

4.6 Claims and Litigation  
To institute, defend, settle, or otherwise resolve any claims, lawsuits, or administrative proceedings involving the Principal’s financial interests, including the power to retain legal counsel.

4.7 Personal and Family Support  
To use the Principal’s funds to provide for the Principal’s health, support, maintenance, and education, and for the support of the Principal’s dependents as the Attorney-in-Fact deems necessary.

5. LIMITATIONS ON AUTHORITY  
Notwithstanding the general authority granted above, the Attorney-in-Fact shall have no authority to:  
a. Make or alter the Principal’s will or trusts;  
b. Consent to or refuse any medical or health care treatment;  
c. Make gifts on behalf of the Principal exceeding One Thousand Dollars ($1,000) per transaction or Five Thousand Dollars ($5,000) per calendar year, unless expressly authorized in writing by the Principal;  
d. Use the Principal’s property for the Attorney-in-Fact’s own benefit except for reasonable compensation for services rendered, if agreed by written contract.

6. THIRD PARTY RELIANCE  
Any person, including financial institutions or other entities, may rely on a photocopy or electronically transmitted copy of this Power of Attorney or any certification provided hereunder, and shall not be liable for any action taken or not taken in reliance on this Power of Attorney.

7. ACCOUNTING AND RECORDS  
The Attorney-in-Fact shall keep full records of all transactions undertaken pursuant to this Power of Attorney and shall provide an accounting to the Principal or the Principal’s legal representative upon request within thirty (30) days of such request.

8. EFFECTIVENESS AND TERMINATION  
This Power of Attorney shall become effective immediately upon execution and shall remain in effect until:  
a. The Principal’s death;  
b. The Principal revokes this Power of Attorney in writing delivered to the Attorney-in-Fact; or  
c. A court of competent jurisdiction declares the Power of Attorney invalid.

9. INDEMNIFICATION  
The Principal agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Attorney-in-Fact from any liability, loss, or expense resulting from acts or omissions made in good faith under this Power of Attorney.

10. GOVERNING LAW  
This Power of Attorney shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Washington.
